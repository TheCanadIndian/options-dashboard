0DTE Lab

Same-day SPX and SPY forward tests at real prices, paper only.

Strategy race0DTE lab

Each variant buys the nearest out-of-the-money 0DTE contract within its price cap, at the real ask, one contract, paper only. Day call: the morning direction call, bought at the open. Trend day: a strong first hour closing near its extreme, bought at about 10:30, with and without a dealers-short-gamma filter. Market profile: at 10:30, the 80% rule (opened outside yesterday's 30-minute value area and back inside it: sold when price reaches the far side of value) and a gap down reclaimed into yesterday's range. Momentum: at 3:30 PM, above every high from 9:30 to 2:30 and the open, a call (below, a put), sold at 3x or held to the close. The backtests behind them showed small edges that real option prices could erase, which is what these records are for.

Each variant is its own paper portfolio of $1,000, one contract a trade, skipped when the portfolio can't pay for it. Day-call contracts are sold at 10:30 when the first hour runs 0.25% or more against the call. Every finished day gets a review: what the market did against the forecasts, why each trade won or lost, and the lessons.
