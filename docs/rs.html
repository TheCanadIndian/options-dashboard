  • RS change: the rating now against 4 weeks ago. Volatility: weekly price swings over 3 months, in thirds across the index.
  • Over 1 to 3 weeks the rating made almost no difference (under 0.4% against SPY), so it does not feed the options scanner.
  • Tested on today's S&P 500 members, weekly from 2008: companies that collapsed and left the index are missing, which flatters the low-rated. One stock beating SPY 54% of the time is close to a coin flip: it is a tilt across many names, not a reason to buy one.
