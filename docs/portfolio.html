  • Starts with $1,000 in cash. Nothing here is real money.
  • Direction comes from a combined read of auction and market profile (value area, point of control), dealer positioning (gamma walls and flip, vanna, charm, and customer delta exposure estimated from classified buy and sell flow), Wyckoff structure, volume price analysis, and trend.   • Buys one contract when the scanner scores it 70 or more out of 100, it costs no more than 15% of the account, and there is cash for it. At most 5 positions, one per stock.
  • Only buys near a level that would prove the trade wrong: the stop sits just beyond the nearest support under a call (or resistance over a put), at most 1 ATR from the price, and the next level in the trade's direction must be at least 2× that far. Sells when the stock crosses the stop or reaches the target level, when the bid is 50% above the purchase price, at a 40% backstop on the option for gaps, or 7 days before expiry.
  • Only trades with the broad market: the average read of SPY and QQQ. When it is beyond ±15, a trade against it needs a read of at least ±50 on its own stock. At most 3 positions bet the same way.
  • Buys only after 10:30 New York time, when spreads have settled, only when the bid/ask spread is 8% or less, and at most 1 per scan and 2 per day.
  • Twice a day (09:45 and 15:30 New York time) every position at least 3 trading days old is re-checked against the current read. It is sold only if the read has reversed against it; a read that has merely gone neutral leaves it to its stop and target.
  • Buys fill at the ask and sells at the bid, with $0.65 commission each way.
  • Prices come from Yahoo Finance, delayed about 15 minutes, and are checked every 15 minutes while the market is open. A real stop order could fill at a different price.
  • After a sale, the same stock is not bought again for 2 days.
